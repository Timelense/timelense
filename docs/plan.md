TimeLens

Your time tells a story.

TimeLens is a productivity and self-awareness platform that helps people understand how they spend their most valuable resource — time.

Unlike traditional time trackers that focus only on logging activities, TimeLens helps users visualize patterns, identify time leaks, measure productivity, and build a healthier relationship with time through meaningful insights and analytics.

🚀 Vision

Most people know where their money goes.

Very few know where their time goes.

TimeLens aims to become the personal analytics dashboard for human time, helping users make data-driven decisions about how they spend their days, weeks, months, and ultimately, their lives.

✨ MVP Features
Start & stop task timer
Task categorization and sub-categorization
Productive vs non-productive tagging
Task notes and details
Daily activity timeline
Time distribution analytics
Productivity scoring
Weekly and monthly insights
Personal time reports
🎯 Problem

People often feel busy but struggle to answer:

Where did my day go?
How much time did I actually spend on productive work?
What activities consume most of my week?
Am I improving my habits over time?

TimeLens transforms raw activity logs into actionable insights.

💡 Core Philosophy

"What gets measured gets managed."

TimeLens helps users see the story their time is telling and empowers them to make intentional decisions about how they live, work, and grow.

🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: React Native
Backend: Node.js
Database: PostgreSQL
Authentication: JWT / OAuth
Analytics Engine: Custom Insight Algorithms
